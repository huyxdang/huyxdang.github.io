Why it matters

Many queries do not need a costly large language model. SMART trains a small model to emit confidence tokens after answering, then escalates uncertain queries. On MATH-500, this approach kept most queries on the smaller model and nearly halved measured inference cost while retaining 98.4% of the orchestrator’s accuracy.

Inference cost refers to the paper’s measured model-serving cost, not total token usage.
